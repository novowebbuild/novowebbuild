Common questions

How much does a website cost?

Custom websites start at €600, landing pages at €250, QR menus at €150 and digital invitations at €120. You get a fixed quote before any work begins, so the price never moves halfway through.

How long does it take?

Most websites go live within 15 working days of receiving your content. Landing pages and QR menus are usually ready in under a week.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

None. You send a logo, photos and a few lines about the business. We take care of design, code, domain, hosting, analytics and launch.

Do you use templates or page builders?

No. Every site is designed and hand-coded for one business. That is why our sites load fast, rank well and do not look like anyone else's.

Do you work with businesses outside Thessaloniki?

Yes. The studio is in Thessaloniki and the work is fully online. We have built for clients across Greece, in Spain and in North Macedonia, in Greek, English, Spanish and Italian.

What happens after the site is live?

You can run it yourself, or choose a maintenance plan from €30 per month that covers hosting support, content updates, security checks and SEO monitoring.
